# Narrative Structure Guide

This guide is meant to guide the structure of the narrative outline and provide examples for how the narrative should be generated. 

# Narrative Overview

The narrative generated should focus on a boss fight between the player character, Elara, and the boss, The Faceless One. It should feature a chase fight scene, where Elara has to put distance between herself and the Faceless One, while using her environment to her advantage to defeat the boss.

# Outline Structure

This describes the structure that the narrative design should follow.

1. Opening scene: a cinematic to be played, introducing the start of the fight scene  
   1. Checkpoint 1: First choice checkpoint with two choices. One will lead to a Game Over state  where Elara dies while the other will proceed to the first progression cinematic  
      1. Checkpoint 1 Game Over Cinematic: Played before the Game Over screen, and reflects the action described in the choice the player made  
2. Progression Scene 1: A cinematic scene played based on the player’s choice, should lead into Checkpoint 2  
   1. Checkpoint 2:  Second choice checkpoint with two choices. One will lead to a Game Over state where Elara dies while the other will proceed to the second progression cinematic  
      1. Checkpoint 2 Game Over Cinematic: Played before the Game Over screen, and reflects the action described in the choice the player made  
3. Progression Scene 2: A cinematic scene played based on the player’s choice, should lead into Checkpoint 3  
   1. Checkpoint 3:  Second choice checkpoint with two choices. One will lead to a Game Over state where Elara dies while the other will proceed to the True Ending cinematic  
      1. Checkpoint 3 Game Over Cinematic: Played before the Game Over screen, and reflects the action described in the choice the player made  
4. True Ending Cinematic: played after Checkpoint 3\. Reflects the narrative choice made in Checkpoint 3 and leads into the narrative end where Elara defeats the boss.

# Narrative Example

This is an example of a progression scene’s narrative style.  
Progression: Elara runs down the hall, the Faceless One just behind her. She stumbles to a stop in front of a hole in the floor boards.  
Choice 1: Jump over the hole to the other side  
Choice 2: Go into the room on the left
