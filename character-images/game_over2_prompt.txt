### **Detailed Breakdown (Why It Works):**

This prompt is structured to give the AI the strongest possible instructions, moving from the core subject to style and technical details.

**1. Subject & Action:**
*   **"cinematic still, gothic horror"**: Immediately sets the genre and format.
*   **"a woman (Elara) trapped in a narrow, claustrophobic industrial ventilation shaft"**: Clearly defines the main subject and her predicament. "Claustrophobic" is a powerful keyword for mood.
*   **"the cold brushed metal walls are contracting and crushing her"**: Describes the critical action and the environment's texture.
*   **"In the darkness ahead, a terrifying face (The Everyman) materializes from glitching, shifting data patterns and holographic static"**: A precise description of the antagonist, combining organic terror ("face") with technological horror ("glitching data," "static").
*   **"its expression is serene and omniscient"**: Captures the chilling whisper from your scene—calm yet all-knowing.

**2. Lighting & Color:**
*   **"illuminated by an eerie, sickly green and cyan bioluminescent glow"**: Establishes the primary color palette common in tech-horror and gothic scenes (think old monitors, necrotic energy).
*   **"casts dramatic shadows"**: Reinforces the horror tone and adds depth.
*   **"Chiaroscuro lighting"**: A technical term for strong contrast between light and dark, essential for a dramatic, classic horror look.
*   **"volumetric fog"**: Adds atmosphere and makes the light beams visible, enhancing the cinematic feel.

**3. Composition & Shot Details:**
*   **"Extreme close-up, Dutch angle"**: The close-up emphasizes Elara's panic and the invading face. The Dutch angle (tilted camera) creates immediate unease and disorientation.
*   **"intense claustrophobic composition"**: Reinforces the feeling of being trapped. The AI will likely frame the shot tightly with walls pressing in from all sides.

**4. Style & Details:**
*   **"Hyper-detailed, intricate gothic machinery, rivets, pipes"**: Directs the AI towards the "Gothic" part of "Gothic Horror," specifying the look of the ventilation shaft.
*   **"Style of Guillermo del Toro, HR Giger, and Dante's Inferno (2007)"**: Provides strong stylistic references:
    *   **Guillermo del Toro**: For the gothic, fantastical, and human horror (e.g., *Pan's Labyrinth*).
    *   **H.R. Giger**: For the biomechanical, industrial, and deeply unsettling aesthetic (*Alien*).
    *   **Dante's Inferno (2007 game)**: For its specific depiction of hellish, gritty, and oppressive environments.
*   **"film grain, hyper-realistic, 8k"**: Adds a cinematic texture and requests the highest detail level.
